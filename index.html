WHO WE ARE

We're music lovers. That's it. We all come for the same reason.

THE LIVING ROOM

Every session starts the same way: a living room, a sound system, and people who care about the music.

THE MUSIC

DJs play what they actually listen to — no safe setlists, no expectations. Just the music they love.

THE PEOPLE

Some of us play. Some of us dance. Some of us just close our eyes and feel it.

THE ARCHIVE

We record everything. Not for content. So everyone who was there can remember what it felt like.

FOR VENUES

We're not just another promoter. We're a content-first collective with a proven track record.

CONTENT PRODUCTION

High-end video and audio recording for every set. We create assets that promote the venue and the artists.

ORGANIC GROWTH

A loyal community that follows the music, not the hype. Real engagement, real people.

INTERNATIONAL REACH

Artists from 5+ countries. We bring a global sound to local spaces.

PROFESSIONALISM
